About

Clear ideas, made visible.

I studied Graphic Design at the University of Education, Winneba, and I've spent the decade since turning complex programmes into things people stop and look at: donor campaigns, 50-page annual reports, brand systems, safety comics for ship crews, and animated films for parents and learners.

At EduSpots I lead the visual identity of an education charity working across Ghana and Kenya. Through Creavia Studios I direct AI films such as Accra Hustle: No Bush Left and teach teams to make content with generative tools. I work remotely, I'm used to UK and US hours, and I hit deadlines.
